Company

One locomotive plus a growing number of one wagon type, run on the route configured on the Route tab, over real simulated years. Revenue posts each time a leg completes; maintenance and loan interest are charged every month, both counted from the moment the initial train is bought. The loan can only be drawn or repaid in exact $500,000 steps — a smaller amount just can't make a payment. Two strategies are simulated side by side: reinvest spends every affordable dollar on another wagon and never touches the loan; pay off first puts everything toward the loan instead, in $500k steps, and buys no wagons at all until it's fully repaid — then behaves exactly like reinvest from that point on.
